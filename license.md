**🚩 CTF Challenge Tracker** is the complete English-language guide to building a Capture The Flag challenge tracker. Learn how to model challenges, track solves, compute scoreboards, add team support, and export results — with runnable JavaScript and a clean dashboard. Built for CTF organizers, security students, and teams who want a lightweight tracker. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
